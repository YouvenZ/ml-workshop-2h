  1. Purpose. This is a teaching competition for participants of the Python & ML Workshop at the American University in Dubai. There are no cash prizes; the reward is learning and bragging rights.

  3. Honesty. Predictions must be produced by a model trained on the provided training data. Submitting answers looked up from public sources, or hand-labelled, is not allowed and will be removed from the leaderboard.

  5. Sharing. Discuss ideas, features and models openly. Do not share prediction files.
  6. Results. The organiser may show the final leaderboard (usernames and scores) in class and in workshop materials.
  7. Changes. The organiser may fix errors in the data or scoring and re-score submissions if needed.

By registering you accept these terms. Questions: ask during the workshop or in the competition forum.
