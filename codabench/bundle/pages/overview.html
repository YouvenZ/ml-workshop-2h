15 April 1912. The RMS Titanic sinks in the North Atlantic. Of the passengers in our data, about 38% survive, and who survives is anything but random.

You have spent the workshop learning split → fit → predict → score. Now use it for real: train a model on 712 passengers whose fate you know, and predict it for 179 passengers the model has never seen. The leaderboard is the test set, scored live.

THE CHALLENGE

Your mission in three moves

🧠
Train
Build any model on train.csv: logistic regression, KNN, a decision tree… Feature ideas welcome.
🔮
Predict
Predict Survived (0 or 1) for every passenger in test.csv.
🏆
Climb
Upload a zip with predictions.csv. Your score, a detailed report and your rank appear in about a minute.
