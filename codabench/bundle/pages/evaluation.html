EVALUATION

How you are scored

Your predictions for the 179 test passengers are compared with what really happened. The leaderboard is ranked by accuracy; two more scores are shown because accuracy alone hides things.

scoreformularead it as
🎯 Accuracy (ranking)(TP + TN) / 179the share of passengers you got right
⚖️ Balanced accuracy(recallsurvived + recalldied) / 2fair to both classes, even though more people died
🛟 F1 (survived)2 · precision · recall / (precision + recall)how well you find the survivors without false alarms

Each submission gets a detailed report (click the 👁 icon next to it): your confusion matrix, your accuracy against the baselines, and your accuracy for women, men and each ticket class. Use it to find where your model is weak.

KEY IDEA
Ties are broken by submission time: the earlier best score ranks higher. The leaderboard shows each participant's best submission.
FAIR PLAY

Rules

  • Up to 10 submissions per day, 60 in total.
  • Predictions must come from a model trained on train.csv. Looking up the real answers is not machine learning.
  • Teams of up to 2 (the workshop pairs) submit under one account.
  • Any library is allowed. Share ideas freely in the forum; don't share prediction files.
